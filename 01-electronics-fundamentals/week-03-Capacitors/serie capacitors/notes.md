## Total Capacitance of Capacitors in Series

This was a relatively simple topic. When capacitors are connected in **series**, the total capacitance is calculated using the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals of each capacitor.

For multiple capacitors:

**1/Cₜ = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + 1/C₃ + ... + 1/Cₙ**

For two capacitors, this can also be simplified to:

**Cₜ = (C₁ × C₂) / (C₁ + C₂)**

Unlike capacitors in parallel, adding the capacitances directly does **not** work when they are connected in series.

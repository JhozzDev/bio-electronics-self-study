### Current-Voltage relationship
It sets that the current is proportional to the voltage's derive multiplied by the capacitance over time.

(\(i(t)=C\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\)

(Where i is current, C is capacitance, and dv/dt is the rate of change of voltage with respect to time.)

### Observations
No Voltage Change means Zero Current: If the voltage across a capacitor is constant (dv/dt = 0), the current is zero. This is why capacitors act as open circuits in steady-state DC circuitS

Continuous Voltage: The voltage across a capacitor cannot change instantaneously. Doing so would require an infinite rate of change (dv/dt), which demands infinite current—a physical impossibility

Proportional Response: A fast-changing voltage ramp creates a constant current, while a steady DC voltage blocks current entirel

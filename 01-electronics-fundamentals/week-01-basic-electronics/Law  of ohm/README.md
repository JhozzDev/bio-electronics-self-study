# What did i learn today

I = Amps

R = Resistance

V = Volts

P = Watts

1kR = 1000R

1ma = 0.001A

## Notes
Volts mean "Electrical potential difference which pushes all the electrics charges"
Ampers mean "Amount of electric charges which flow by time unit"
Resistances mean "Opposition to the current's flow"

When the resistances are in  serie the total resistance(RT) is equal to all the resistances together. 
RT = R1 + R2 + R3
When the circuit is in paralel the total Amp(IT) is equal to all the amp together. 
IT = I1 + I2 + I3

Voltage → push
Current → flow
Resistance → opposition
Power → rate of energy consumption

### Volts

V = IR
V = P/I
V = sqrt(PxR)

### Amps
I = P/V
I = V/R

### Resistance
R = V/I
R = P/(I)2
R = (V)2/P

### Watts
P = R x (I)2
P = I x V
P = (V)2/R

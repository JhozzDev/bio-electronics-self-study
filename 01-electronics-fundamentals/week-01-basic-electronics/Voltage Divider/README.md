## Important Concepts

### [Voltage Divider]

[A voltage divider is a simple electronic circuit that turns a large input voltage into a smaller output voltage. It uses two or more resistors connected in a line (in series) across a power source.]

### [Equivalent Resistance]

[Equivalent resistance is the total resistance of a single imaginary resistor that can replace a group of connected resistors without changing the overall flow of current or voltage in a circuit]

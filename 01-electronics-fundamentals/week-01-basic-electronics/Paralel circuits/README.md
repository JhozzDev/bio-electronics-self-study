## What I Learned

In a Parallel Circuit, the voltage still being the same in every branch therefore allows each line work independently without depending on the other. each and every lines has its autonomy.
to Find the total resistance you can use the formula or calculate V/It

## Important Concepts

### [Voltage]
It's constant, every device receives the initial Voltage, no matter how man they are

### [Amperes]
It gets divided, letting every branch consume the exact amount is supposed to

### [Total resistance]
Decreases, it's necessary lower than the lowest resistance

## Formulas

Voltage = Vt = V1 = V2 = V3 (it doesn't change)
Amperes = It = I1 + I2 + I3 (it divided in eery branch and the it's sum)
Total Resistance = RT = 1/r1+1/r2+1/r3... (It's lower than the lowest resistance)
